The math behind it

To move n disks, first move the top n − 1 disks out of the way, move the largest disk, then move the n − 1 disks back on top of it. So the minimum number of moves satisfies T(n) = 2·T(n − 1) + 1 with T(1) = 1, which solves to T(n) = 2ⁿ − 1. No strategy can do better, because the largest disk must move at least once, and each time it moves the other n − 1 disks must all be stacked on the third peg.
